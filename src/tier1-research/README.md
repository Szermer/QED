# Tier 1: Research Collection

This directory contains **raw research material** captured from various sources. These articles are in their original state and have not been formally analyzed or validated.

## Status: Raw Research

These articles:

- Have been captured from their original sources
- Include source URLs for attribution
- May contain formatting artifacts from the original sites
- Have NOT been professionally evaluated
- Have NOT been validated in production

## Processing Pipeline

1. **Tier 1 (This Directory)**: Raw captured articles with source attribution
2. **Tier 2 (src/analysis/)**: Professional evaluation and risk assessment
3. **Tier 3 (src/patterns/)**: Proven practices validated in production

## Organization

- **high-priority/**: Articles requiring immediate evaluation
- **medium-priority/**: Articles for future analysis
- **low-priority/**: Articles for reference only
- **archive/**: Outdated or superseded research

## Note on Formatting

These articles maintain their original formatting, which may include:

- YAML frontmatter from source sites
- Minor formatting inconsistencies
- Various markdown styles

This is intentional - Tier 1 serves as an archive of source material. Professional formatting and analysis occurs in Tier 2.

For analyzed content with risk assessments and recommendations, see the [Tier 2 Analysis](../analysis/) directory.
